How editions work

Start with published, platform-authored articles for a reader edition, or choose a private editorial proof to include drafts. Imported RSS stories are not eligible. Your selection is saved as a snapshot. Refresh a working edition explicitly to pull newer article text; finalizing locks its text, order and layout.

Every edition starts private. A finalized published-content edition can be shared separately. If any source is withdrawn, the entire shared issue becomes unavailable, including its images. A private archive keeps its captured text. Printed and downloaded copies cannot be recalled. Images that cannot be safely archived are omitted with a notice.

Preview is approximate: browser print preview controls pagination. Choose the same paper size, 100% scale, and disable browser headers and footers. Use the preview’s Print button to select a printer or Save as PDF. No physical print job is sent automatically.
